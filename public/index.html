⚡ Our Pitch

A gamified, Pokémon Go-style civic radar that publicly maps local issues to prevent duplicate reporting and build transparency.

🛑 The Problem We Solve

Citizens and GVMC waste time on duplicate, unverified complaints because there is no shared, location-based public map of local issues.

💡 Our Solution

For daily commuters who face civic hazards, Vizag Go is a map-based reporting tool that plots nearby problems on a real-time radar. Unlike official government portals, we use crowdsourced visibility to prevent duplicate reports and build community transparency.

✨ What Makes Us Different

None provide a publicly visible, real-time map of local issues to prevent duplicate reporting.
